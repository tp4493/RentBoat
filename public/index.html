Making a reservation is simple and flexible!

You can choose the way that suits you best:

• Direct Email: Send us an email anytime at gstamathioudakis@gmail.com — we typically respond within a few hours.

Before departure, we also provide you with a full briefing and instructions to ensure you feel confident and safe during your trip.

Your safety comes first. If the weather conditions are unsafe, we will either reschedule your booking for another day or offer you a full refund — your choice.

We recommend bringing sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, swimwear, towels, and a light jacket. Don’t forget your camera to capture the amazing views! Water and snacks are also a good idea.

We recommend arriving at least 15–20 minutes before your scheduled time. This allows time for a quick briefing, paperwork, and a smooth departure without any rush.

Fuel is usually charged separately based on the amount used during your trip. Before setting off, we will explain everything clearly so there are no surprises when you return.
